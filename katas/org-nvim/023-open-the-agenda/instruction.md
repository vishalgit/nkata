# Open the agenda

## Goal
See your week, and read what the agenda tells you.

## What to do
- Add this file to the agenda: `<C-c>[` (once per session).
- `<prefix>a` then `a`. Press `gd` and go to 2026-10-05.
- Try `vd` (day), `vw` (week), `vm` (month), `f` later, `b` earlier, `.` today.
- `<prefix>a` then `t` lists every open TODO.
- Fill in the answers after each `::` from what you saw.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- The conference spans three days; the agenda marks each day like `(1/3)`.
- Look for the habit graph and the deadline countdown ("In N d.") too.
