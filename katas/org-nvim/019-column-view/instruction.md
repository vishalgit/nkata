# Column view

## Goal
See headlines as rows in a table.

## What to do
- On `Write the quarterly report` press `<prefix>C`.
- Move to the Effort cell of `Gather the numbers` and press `n` twice: 1:00 → 2:00 → 4:00.
- On `Prepare the slides` press `n` once: 0:30 → 1:00.
- Watch the parent total (5:00). Press `q` to quit.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- `p` steps back, `e` edits a cell by hand, `<CR>` jumps to the entry.
