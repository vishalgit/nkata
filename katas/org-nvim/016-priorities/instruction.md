# Priorities

## Goal
Rank tasks A, B, C.

## What to do
- On `Review the pull request` press `<S-Up>`. It becomes A.
- On `Clean up the downloads folder` press `<prefix>,` then `<Space>`. The priority goes.
- On `No priority yet` press `<prefix>,` then `a`. Then `<S-Down>` twice. It ends on C.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- Play with `<S-Up>`/`<S-Down>` on the first task, but leave it at A.
