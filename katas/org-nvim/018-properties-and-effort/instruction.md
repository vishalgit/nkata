# Properties and effort

## Goal
Attach data to a headline.

## What to do
- On `Prepare the slides` press `<prefix>p`, set `CLIENT` to `Acme`.
- Still there, press `<prefix>xe` and pick `0:30`. The choices come from `Effort_ALL`.
- On the `:Effort:` line of `Gather the numbers` press `<S-Right>` once. 1:00 becomes 2:00.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- `<prefix>lI` gives a headline a unique ID. Try it, look, then `u` (an ID is random, so it isn't checked).
- If properties are written with extra spaces, the check wants one space after the key.
