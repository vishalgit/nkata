# Sort, narrow, convert

## Goal
Three small powers.

## What to do
- Convert: on `Reading list` press `<prefix>*`. It becomes a headline.
- Sort: on `Books` press `<prefix>hs` then `a` (alphabetical). Look. Again with `p` (priority).
- Narrow: on `Books` press `<prefix>hn`. The subtree opens alone. `<C-c>'` saves and closes.
- Convert: on `milk` press `<prefix>-`. It becomes a list item.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- `<prefix>*` again on a headline turns it back into a plain line.
