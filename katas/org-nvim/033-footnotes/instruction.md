# Footnotes

## Goal
Add side notes.

## What to do
- Put the cursor on the last `d` of `extend` and press `<prefix>if`.
  The mark should land right after the word. Write `It runs on Neovim too.`
- Press `<CR>` on a footnote mark to jump to its text, and again to come back.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- `4<prefix>if` opens a menu: sort, renumber, normalize, delete.
- If org.nvim places the definition with no blank line, add one to match.
