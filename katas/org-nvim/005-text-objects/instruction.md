# Grab with text objects

## Goal
Delete, yank or select a whole section in one move.

| Object      | Means                            |
|-------------|----------------------------------|
| `ih` `ah`   | section body / with its headline |
| `ir` `ar`   | subtree body / with its headline |

## What to do
- On `Chapter B` press `dar`. The whole subtree is gone (child too).
- On `Chapter A` press `yih` to copy only its body.
- Go to `Chapter C` and press `p` to paste the body under it.

## Help
- Try `dah` on a parent and see that only the section goes, not its children. Then `u`.
