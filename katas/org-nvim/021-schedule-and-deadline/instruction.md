# Schedule and deadline

## Goal
Say "start on" and "must finish by".

## What to do
- On `Dentist appointment` remove the date with `4<C-c><C-s>`.
- Read the `-10d` on the tax task: "warn me 10 days before". Leave it.
- On `Something to plan` press `<prefix>s` and pick 2026-10-30.
- Then `<prefix>d` and pick 2026-11-06.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- In the calendar: `h j k l` move a day/week, `H L` a month, `.` today, `<CR>` picks.
- Org keeps SCHEDULED and DEADLINE on one line. If org.nvim puts them in the other order, swap them by hand.
- If removing the date leaves an empty line, delete it.
