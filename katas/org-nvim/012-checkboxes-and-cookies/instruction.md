# Checkboxes and cookies

## Goal
Tick boxes and watch the counters move.

## What to do
- On `Passport` press `<C-Space>`. The parent shows `[-]`. Press it again to untick.
- Select all three children with `V` and press `<C-Space>`. All ticked, parent `[X]`.
- On `Toothbrush` press `<M-S-CR>` and type `Adapter`. A new empty box.
- Under the headline checklist, tick `Draft the outline` and `Proofread`.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- If a cookie looks stale, press `<prefix>#` to refresh it.
