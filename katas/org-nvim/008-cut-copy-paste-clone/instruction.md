# Cut, copy, paste, clone

## Goal
Move whole subtrees, and repeat a meeting for free.

## What to do
- On `Planning poker` press `<prefix>hd` to cut it.
- On `Retro` press `<prefix>hp` to paste it. It should sit after `Retro`.
- On `Team sync` press `<prefix>hc` (clone). Answer `3` copies, shift `+1w`.

## Help
- `<prefix>` means `<leader>o`. `u` undoes, `<leader>kr` resets the kata.
- `<prefix>hy` copies a subtree instead of cutting it.
- If the paste lands above `Retro`, move it down with `<M-j>`.
- If `Someday` is left empty, that is fine. It stays.
