# 04 Capture (step 1 of GTD)

Capture means: get it out of your head and into the Inbox. Do not decide
anything yet. Do not sort. Just write.

## Goal

Add four new items to the Inbox, as fast as you can.

## Steps

1. Open `start.org`. The Inbox has one item.
2. Put the cursor on `** Call the dentist`.
3. Press `<M-CR>`. A new headline appears below, at the same level.
4. Type `Buy printer paper` and press `<Esc>`.
5. Repeat for these two, in this order:
   - `Reply from the landlord about the heating`
   - `Write a blog post about org mode`

## Tip

`<M-CR>` is the fastest way to add a headline. In Insert mode it also works.

## Bonus (not checked)

`<prefix>c` opens the capture menu, so you can add to the Inbox from any file.
You will set that up in kata 14.

## Done when

Your file matches `expected.org`.
