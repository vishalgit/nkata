# Headings

## Goal
Type the outline shown in the final result pane, starting from a blank editor.

## What to do
- Two top-level headings: `Groceries` and `Errands`.
- `Groceries` has two children: `Fruit` and `Vegetables`.
- `Errands` has one child: `Post office`.

## Help
- A heading is one or more `*` at the very start of the line, then a space.
- One star is level 1, two stars is level 2.
- If org.nvim gives you a mapping to insert a heading or promote/demote one,
  try it here instead of typing the stars by hand.
- Trailing spaces and blank lines at the end don't count.
